Castmill OS installer for x86-64 PCs
====================================

1. Optional: to configure Wi-Fi, a proxy or the time zone, copy
   castmill.conf.example to castmill.conf on this drive and edit it.
   (On Windows, make sure the name doesn't end in ".txt".)

2. Plug this stick into the PC and boot from it (boot menu: usually
   F7, F10, F11 or F12 at power-on). Secure Boot must be off.

3. The installer shows the internal disk it will use and starts after
   10 seconds. Press any key to pick another disk or cancel.
   EVERYTHING ON THAT DISK IS ERASED.

4. When it's done, the PC powers off. Remove the stick and power the PC
   on: Castmill OS starts and every screen shows a registration code.
   Register the screens in the Castmill dashboard.

The same stick installs any number of PCs.

Documentation: https://castmill.github.io/castmill-os/install/intel-x86-64
